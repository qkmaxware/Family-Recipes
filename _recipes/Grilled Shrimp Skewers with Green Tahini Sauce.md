## Instructions
1. Tahini sauce: mix together garlic, lemon, jalapeño, water, white wine vinegar, tahini, and salt into a blender and blend.
2. Heat grill until hot, toss shrimp with olive oil and season with salt and pepper. Arrange on skewers.
3. Grill for 2 minutes each side until shrimp is pink.
4. Serve with Green Tahini sauce on the side
