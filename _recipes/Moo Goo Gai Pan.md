## Instructions
1. salt and pepper the chicken, cook until done. Take out of fry pan.
2. add tbsp olive oil and carrots, sauté. Add mushrooms and sauté 1-2 minutes. Add chestnuts and snow peas, cook 1-2 minutes.
3. Mix together sauce ingredients - brown sugar, garlic, soy sauce, chicken broth, hoisin sauce, cornstarch, water)
4. Add chicken and sauce to fry pan and cook until sauce is thickened.
