## Instructions
1. Chow mein sauce - in a small bowl, whisk sauce ingredients together and set aside,
2. Cook chicken - Heat a large pan or wok on high heat. Add 1 tbsp. oil. Season chicken with salt and pepper and add to hot pan. Sauté until cooked through, then remove to a plate.
3. Cook veggies - add another tablespoon of oil. Once hot, add cabbage, celery, and the whites of the chopped green onion. Stir fry over high heat for 1-2 minutes. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 30 seconds then remove everything to a plate.
4. Noodles - cook noodles according to instructions.
