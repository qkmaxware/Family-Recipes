## Instructions
1. In a small bowl, mix chilli sauce, soy sauce, rice vinegar, and sesame oil. Set aside.
2. Cook noodles according to package. Drain and set aside.
3. Heat vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium high heat. Add garlic and ginger, sauté until fragrant (about 1-2 minutes). 
4. Add the beef and cook for 4-5 minutes until browned. Season with salt and pepper. Stir in the chilli sauce mixture and cook for another 2-3 minutes until the sauce thickens.
5. Toss in the cooked noodles, carrots, Napa cabbage and green onions. Stir well to coat everything in the sauce. Garnish with sesame seeds and chili flakes.
