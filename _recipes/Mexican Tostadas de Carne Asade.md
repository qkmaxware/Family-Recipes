## Instructions
1. Marinade the carne aside: In a bowl, combine olive oil, garlic, lime juice, cumin, chilli powder, salt, and pepper. Rub the mixture over the steak and let it marinade from 30 minutes to 2 hours.
2. Heat a grill pan or skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the steak for 4-5 minutes per side or until desired doneness is achieved. Let the steak rest for 5 minutes, then slice it thinly against the grain.
3. Assemble the tostados: spread a layer of guacamole on each tostada shell. Top with slices of carne aside. Add pico de Gallo and any additional toppings you like.
