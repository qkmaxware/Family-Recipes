## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees. Cream butter and sugar until fluffy. Add pumpkin, egg and vanilla and mix well.
2. Combine flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, cinnamon and nutmeg. Stir into butter mixture until well blended. Add nuts and raisons. Drop by teaspoonful onto parchment paper covered baking sheet, about 2 inches apart. Bake about 15 minutes or until golden. Cool.
3. Cream icing sugar and butter. Add remaining icing ingredients and beat until smooth. Drizzle over cookies
