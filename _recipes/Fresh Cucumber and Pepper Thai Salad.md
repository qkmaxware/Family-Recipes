## Instructions
1. slice cucumbers into paper thin rounds. Slice the peppers into thin strips. Put the peppers, cucumbers, and jalapeño peppers into a bowl.
2. Combine the rest of the ingredients. Add to the salad bowl. Toss well.
3. Let stand for at least one hour in the refrigerator before serving
