## Instructions
1. Heat the sesame oil in a skillet, over medium heat. Cook the ground beef until browned.
2. Drain excess grease.
3. Add diced bell peppers and onion, Saute for 4-5 minutes until softened.
4. Stir in soya sauce, hoisin sauce, garlic powder, ginger, and red papper flakes. Cook for 2-3 minutes.
5. Add cooked rice.
