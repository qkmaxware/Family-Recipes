## Instructions
1. Cook spaghetti as per instructions.
2. In a stirfry skillet, cook ground beef, add carrots near the end and cook for a few minutes.
3. Mix together soy sauce, brown sugar, hoisin sauce, beef broth, ginger, garlic, black pepper and red pepper flakes. Add to the skillet along with spaghetti. Stir together until combined. Add green onion.
