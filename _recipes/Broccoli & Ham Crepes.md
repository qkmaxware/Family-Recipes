## For Crepes
1. Combine eggs and salt in a medium bowl. Gradually add flour alternately with milk, beating with an electric mixer. Beat or whisk in oil.
2. Refrigerate batter for at least 30 minutes. Batter may thicken, so if necessary, add a bit more milk to thin.
3. spray 8 inch non-skick fillet with cooking spray. Heat over medium high heat until water droplets sizzle when sprinkled into skillet.
4. Stir batter. Pour about 3 tbsp. batter into skillet all at once and quickly tilt and rotate the skillet to coat the bottom with batter.
5. Cook until bottom is slightly browned, about 45 seconds. Turn crepe over with spatula. Cook for 15-30 seconds on the second side and transfer to a plate. Repeat until all batter is gone.
6. Add toppings and roll up. Place in an over-proof high-edged baking dish. Bake for 400 degrees for about 30 minutes.
7. Cook broccoli or asparagus until slightly tender.
8. spread small amount of ham or smoked turkey along middle of crepe and add vegetable to the middle as well. Roll the crepe and layer them into a casserole dish.
9. Once all crepes have been layered, bake in 375 degree oven for about 30 minutes.
