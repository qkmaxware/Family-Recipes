## Instructions
1. Add flour, baking powder, sugar and salt into a bowl. Stir to mix.
2. Add milk, egg, and vegetable oil. Blend together with a mixer.
3. Spray vegetable oil onto a skillet and heat. When hot, scoop batter into pan. When it starts to bumble good, flip pancakes to the other side and cook for another minute or so until lightly brown.
