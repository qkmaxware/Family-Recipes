## Instructions
1. heat 2 tbsp olive oil in skillet over medium heat, cook mushrooms
2.drizzle 1 tbsp olive oil over rolled out pizza dough or flatbread; bake 10-15 minutes at 500 degrees
3.toss arugula with lemon oil and salt; add to top of pizza
