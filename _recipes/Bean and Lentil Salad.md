## Instructions
1. If using dried beans, prepare by boiling and draining. If using canned, drain and set aside.
2. In a large bowl, combine peppers, tomatoes, Chile, thyme, and garlic. Mix well.
3. In a small bowl, whisk together vinegar, oil, pepper and salt. Pour over the pepper-tomato mixture.
4. Add cold beans to the pepper tomato mixture and mix well to coat. Serve over lettuce or spinach.
