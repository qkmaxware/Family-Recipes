## Instructions
1. Tear spinach into bite size pieces. Place in large salad bowl. Scatter radishes, carrot and onion, carrots, and radishes over top. (optionally - can also add cherry tomatoes and sliced hardboiled egg)
2. In a separate bowl, mix together remaining ingredients for the dressing.
3. drizzle dressing on salad, toss and coat
