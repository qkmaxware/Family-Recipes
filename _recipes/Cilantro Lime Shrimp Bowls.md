## Instructions
1. Add oil and garlic in a large sauce pan. Heat to medium/high heat.
2. Add in shrimp and sauté for 5-10 minutes until shrimp is pink.
3. Squeeze on lime juice, Eason with salt, pepper, cumin and cilantro
