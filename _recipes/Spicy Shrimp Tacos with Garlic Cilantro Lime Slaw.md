## Instructions
1. Add oil, water, green onions, cilantro leaves, 2 cloves garlic, salt, juice of two limes and either sour cream or yogurt into a blender and blend till smooth.
2. Mix together spices and sea salt. Pat the shrimp dry with paper towels. Toss in a small bowl with the spices and coat. Heat a drizzle of oil to a large skillet over medium high heat and cook shrimp for 5-8 minutes until pink.
3. Toss some of the garlic cilantro lime sauce with the cabbage (not all of it, as rest will go on top of tacos.
4. assemble shrimp, slaw, and toppings (avacado, cheese, and lime juice in a taco and drizzle some garlic cilantro lime sauce on top.
5. Serve
