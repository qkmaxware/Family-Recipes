## Instructions
1. In a medium bowl, whisk together rice vinegar, soy sauce, lime juice, honey, and garlic. Add the peanut butter and red pepper flakes. Stir until smooth.
2. In a large bowl, combine all ingredients except the wraps. Divide the mixture over the wraps and drizzle peanut sauce on top. roll up the tortillas and cut in half before serving.
