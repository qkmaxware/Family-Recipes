## Instructions
1. Drain and rinse black beans. In a large bowl, combine beans with prepared  vegetables, fruits and cilantro.
2. Whisk together vinegar, lime juice, sherry, oil, salt and pepper. Drizzle over salad. Toss well to mix.
3. Let salad chill for 2 hours, stirring occasionally. Serve garnished with tortilla chips
