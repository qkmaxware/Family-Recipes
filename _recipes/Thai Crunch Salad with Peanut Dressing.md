## Instructions
1. Combine peanut butter, rice vinegar, lime juice, oil, soy sauce, honey, sugar, garlic cloves, ginger, salt, red pepper flakes and 2 tbsp of cilantro in a blender and blend till smooth.
2. mix together cabbage, carrots, peppers, cucumber, edamame, scallions and 1/2 cup loosely packed cilantro.
3. pour dressing on salad.
