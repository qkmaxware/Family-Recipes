## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees Fahrenheit. Grease casserole dish.
2. Mix together flower, oats, sugar, nutmeg, and cinnamon in a bowl.
3. Cut in margarine until crumbly.
4. Arrange sliced apples in a casserole dish. Sprinkle lemon juice over the apples. Sprinkle rolled oat mixture over apples. Bake for 10 minutes.
