## Instructions
1. Toss the vegetables with 1 tsp olive oil
2. Place vegetables on a baking sheet to broil or directly on the barbecue, and grill until slightly blackened
2. Whisk dressing ingredients together
3. Toss in vegetables and serve at room temperature
