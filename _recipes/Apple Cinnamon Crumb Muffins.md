## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees F. Line muffin tin with paper lines (approximately. 14 muffins).
2. For the crumb topping, mix your sugars and cinnamon, stir in melted butter, then gently fork in the flour. Don't overtax.
3. Whisk together your dry ingredients in one bowl. In other, cream your butter and sugars until fluffy (about 2 minutes.)
4. add eggs, yogurt (or sour cream) and vanilla to your butter mixture. Mix well, then add dry ingredients and milk alternately.
5. fold in the apples gently to distribute them evenly.
6. Fill the muffin cups right to the top, then sprinkle crumb topping on top.
7. Bake for 5 minutes, then reduce heat to 350 degrees and bake for another 15-18 minutes
