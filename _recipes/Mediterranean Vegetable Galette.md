## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F. Roll out the puff pastry on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
2. Arrange zucchini, bell pepper, onion,cherry tomatoes, and olives in the centre of the pastry, leaving a border around the edges.
3. Sprinkle crumbled feta over the vegetables and drizzle with olive oil. Season with oregano, salt, and pepper.
4. Fold the edges of the pastry over the vegetables to create a rustic galette shape. Brush the crust with beaten egg.
5. Bake for 30-35 minutes, until the crust is golden and the vegetables are tender.
