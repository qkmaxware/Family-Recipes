## Instructions
1. Prepare rice as per instructions.
2. In a saucepan, combine all sauce ingredients and bring to boil on medium heat. Cook for 5 minutes, then set aside.
3. Marinade the beef slices with half of the sauce for 30 minutes.
4. Heat oil in a frypan on medium high heat. Stir in beef and cook till it's done. Add the remaining sauce and serve.
